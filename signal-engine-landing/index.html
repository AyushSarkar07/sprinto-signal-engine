Does Signal Engine email real prospects?

No. A person signs off each draft, and only the operator's own test inbox can receive it.

Why are none of the 5 companies leads?

Two run Vanta trust centres and two already hold SOC 2. Saying so is the job.

What if Signal Engine cannot read a site?

The answer is marked unknown and a person decides. The account is never ruled out for it.

Does Signal Engine use Claude?

When a key is set. Otherwise a rule-based reader runs, and each result says which.

Who built Signal Engine?

A student build for MESA Case 02, not affiliated with Sprinto.
